About

Defending Digital India

RJ Nehra is an experienced Cyber Security and IT Infrastructure Professional with over 7 years of expertise spanning cybersecurity operations, network administration, digital forensics, virtualization technologies, server management, and enterprise infrastructure security.

He holds a Master's degree in Mathematics along with advanced qualifications and certifications in Cyber Security, Ethical Hacking, Cyber Forensics, and Information Security. He has been recognized as a Silver Medallist in CII SECEX 2025 for excellence in cybersecurity and technical operations.

He possesses strong technical knowledge in designing, securing, and managing enterprise-level IT environments across both Windows and Linux platforms — with hands-on experience working with firewalls, virtualization platforms, DNS servers, domain environments, secure network architecture, enterprise servers, and cybersecurity monitoring solutions.

Apart from technical administration, RJ Nehra regularly conducts awareness sessions, workshops, online training programmes, and social media campaigns to educate people about cyber threats, phishing attacks, digital fraud prevention, and secure online practices.
