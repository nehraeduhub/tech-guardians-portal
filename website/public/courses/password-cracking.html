Step-by-Step Modules
Zero to Password Cracking Expert

Every module builds on the last. Complete the course and you will be able to execute and defend against real-world password attacks — independently.

W1
Foundations — Passwords & Cryptography
How passwords are stored · Hashing vs encryption · MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, bcrypt, Argon2 · Salting & peppering · Where hashes live (SAM, /etc/shadow, NTDS.dit) · Lab: Identify hash types with hash-identifier

Wordlists & Dictionary Attacks
